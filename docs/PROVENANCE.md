# Project provenance and independent development

Life Around Us is a community development project influenced by **Sony Life with PlayStation** (original PS3 software) and by the independent Windows revival **[kojimasfoxhound/life-around-us](https://github.com/kojimasfoxhound/life-around-us)**. The third-party revival's author and project remain independent; this repository does not claim to own or have authored the upstream work or the original Sony software.

The web/Android ports were locally adapted to the owner's installed source/resources; the PS5, PS4, PS3, and Samsung Tizen code paths have distinct statuses in the roadmap. Screenshots and the demo video are captures of the locally running web experience or an owner-authorized PS5 preview. Different codebases and assets can have different licenses; **a public repository does not automatically grant a license to reproduce third-party copyrighted content**.

Do not include original Sony NPIA00002 PKG files, unmodified Sony executables, decrypted system software, Windows binary build kits, personal device secrets, or signing keys in this repository. Graphics, music, fonts and images not independently licensed for distribution must be supplied privately by the rights holder, or replaced with appropriately licensed assets.
