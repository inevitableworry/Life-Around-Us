# Live camera images of each city's skyline: "City Name = image URL", optionally
# followed by:
#   | crop=0.36-0.60   keep that horizontal slice of a wide panorama
#   | maxage=6         hours; an older image counts as offline
#   | dated=1          refuse a picture with no date (offline placeholders)
#   | referer=url      sent with the request
#   | youtube=<id>     a 24/7 YouTube live stream: its preview picture is used
#                      only while the stream is live right now
# Every camera is fetched at start-up and again with each 3-hour refresh, in
# the background. When a camera is down, stale, or its picture has not
# changed since the last refresh, the city shows its skyline photo instead
# (app/data/city_photos, credits in CITY_PHOTO_CREDITS.md).
# Only cameras that show the city's skyline or a wide view over the city are
# listed. The YouTube streams were checked on 2026-11-16 (live, and their
# picture updating); a stream that ends simply falls back to the photo.
